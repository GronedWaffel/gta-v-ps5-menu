Load the controller-operated menu into GTA V on your jailbroken PS5.

  1. Run the jailbreak and start PS5Debug-NG 1.3.2 once.
  2. Open GTA V, enter Story Mode and load your character.
  3. Open this page on the same PS5 and select Load GTA V menu.
